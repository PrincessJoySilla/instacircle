      How to Download Your Instagram Data

      Follow this step-by-step guide to get the JSON files needed by InstaCircle.

      Before you start

      You need access to your Instagram account. You do not need to share your password with InstaCircle.

      01

      Open Instagram

      Open the Instagram app and go to your profile. Tap the menu icon (☰) in the upper-right corner.

      02

      Open Accounts Center

      Go to Accounts Center. You can usually find it near the top of the Settings and activity page.

      03

      Find the download option

      Select Your information and permissions, then choose Download your information or Download or transfer information, depending on the version of Instagram you see.

      04

      Select your Instagram account

      If you have multiple accounts connected to Accounts Center, select the Instagram account whose followers and following you want to analyze.

      05

      Choose the information to download

      Select Some of your information if that option is available. Look for Followers and following under the connections section.

      If Instagram presents different selection screens, make sure Followers and Following are both included.

      06

      Set the download options

      • Download to device: Choose this option.
      • Date range: Select All time.
      • Format: Choose JSON, not HTML.
      • Media quality: This is not important for InstaCircle.
      07

      Request your download

      Review your selections and submit the request. Instagram may take some time to prepare your information. You may receive a notification or email when the download is ready.

      08

      Download and extract the ZIP file

      Once your download is ready, download the ZIP file to your device. Find it in your Downloads folder, then extract it.

      On Windows, right-click the ZIP file and select Extract All.

      09

      Find the correct JSON files

      Open the extracted folder and look for a folder named connections, then followers_and_following.

      Inside, look for files similar to these:

      📄 followers_1.json

      📄 followers_2.json (if present)

      📄 following.json

      Instagram may split your followers into multiple files. If you have more than one followers JSON file, you must select all of them.

      10

      Upload your files to InstaCircle

      Return to the Upload section:

      • Under Followers JSON, select every followers JSON file.
      • Under Following JSON, select your following.json file.
      • Click Analyze My Circle.

      Your results will appear in the Results section.

      Having trouble finding the files?

      Instagram sometimes changes its menus and folder names. Look for the Followers and Following data in your downloaded export. Make sure you selected JSON format.

      ♡ Your privacy matters.

      InstaCircle processes the JSON files you select directly in your browser. This version does not upload your files to an InstaCircle server or store them on our servers. Your Instagram password is never requested. Any CSV you export is downloaded to your own device.
